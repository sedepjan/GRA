Kyrgyz Samsy

Samsy is a traditional baked pastry enjoyed throughout Kyrgyzstan and Central Asia. It is usually made with flaky dough wrapped around a savory filling of meat and onion. The pastries are commonly shaped into triangles or squares and baked until the outside becomes crisp and golden. Samsy can be served as a filling snack, lunch, or part of a family meal.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound puff pastry or homemade samsy dough
  • 1 pound beef or lamb, finely diced
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Sesame seeds, optional

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Combine the diced meat and chopped onions in a large bowl.
  3. Add the salt, black pepper, and cumin, then mix the filling thoroughly.
  4. Divide the dough into equal portions and roll each portion into a thin circle or square.
  5. Place a spoonful of the meat filling in the center of each piece of dough.
  6. Fold the dough around the filling and firmly pinch the edges closed to form a triangle or square.
  7. Place the samsy seam-side down on a baking sheet.
  8. Brush the tops with beaten egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds if desired.
  9. Bake for about 25 to 35 minutes, or until the pastry is golden brown and the filling is fully cooked.
  10. Allow the samsy to cool for a few minutes before serving.
